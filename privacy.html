Privacy Policy - Razer n8n Ledger

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Who uses this app

Razer n8n Ledger is a personal automation used only by its owner, on the owner's own computer. It is not offered to other users.

What Google data it accesses

  • Gmail: reads transaction receipt emails and their image attachments in the owner's inbox, and deletes those emails after they have been recorded.
  • Google Sheets: appends one row per transaction to the owner's own spreadsheet.

How the data is used

Receipt images are analysed by an AI model running locally on the owner's computer to extract the date, amount, account and transaction type. The results are written only to the owner's own Google Sheets spreadsheet.

Sharing and storage

No data is sold, shared with, or transferred to any third party. Data is not used for advertising. Apart from the owner's own Gmail and Google Sheets, data is stored only on the owner's computer.

Google API Services User Data Policy

This app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time from the Google Account's security settings under third-party connections.
