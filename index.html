Razer n8n Ledger

Razer n8n Ledger is a private, single-user automation that runs on the owner's own laptop using n8n.

It reads bank and e-wallet transaction receipts from the owner's own Gmail inbox, extracts the transaction details with a locally running AI model, and records them in the owner's own Google Sheets spreadsheet for personal bookkeeping.

This app is not offered to the public and is used only by its owner.
